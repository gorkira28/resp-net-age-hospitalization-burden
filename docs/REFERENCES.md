## Source interpretation notes

CDC describes RESP-NET as population-based surveillance for laboratory-confirmed influenza-, COVID-19-, and RSV-associated hospitalizations in selected counties. The network covers approximately 12% of the U.S. population. Rates are unadjusted and can be affected by clinical testing practices and diagnostic sensitivity; recent estimates are preliminary and subject to revision.
